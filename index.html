Resources for Learning:

Please feel free to tinker and peruse my creations. These are things I've developed to assist me as a teacher, to help young people learn or just for fun.

  • Story card game

    Sort the sentences, spot the odd one out, and build your own story.

    Learning objective: Learning new words, developing knowledge of story elements

  • Writing for Father's Day

    Downloadable templates: a sentence builder and a draft card template.

    Learning objective: Learning new words, developing knowledge of story elements

  • Analog to Digital (First Worksheet)

    A blank template for modelling analog to digital time with Year 2 students.

    Learning objective: Reading and writing quarter past, half past and quarter to on analog and digital clocks

  • Digital Time in Quarter Hours Worksheet

    A printable worksheet: read five analog clocks and write each time in digital format and in words.

    Learning objective: Reading quarter past, half past and quarter to on an analog clock and writing the time in digital format and in words

  • Going from Digital to Analog

    A printable worksheet: read the digital clock and draw the hands on the analog clock. With an extension sheet.

    Learning objective: Reading o'clock and half past on a digital clock and drawing the hour and minute hands on an analog clock

Edi

Writing practice I built during my teaching practicum for learners who need some extra scaffolding. The teacher types sentences or word chains, and the learner builds each word from sound tiles, then traces it. It's designed so a child can work on their own while the teacher is with the rest of the class.

Objective: Spelling words by their sounds (letters, digraphs and trigraphs), forming letters correctly, and writing whole sentences with capital letters and punctuation

Open full screen

How to use it

  • Tap Writing Practice and type one sentence or word chain per line, or tap one of the Writing Starters (sentences, and word chains such as ow, oo, aw, -ing and -ed).
  • Press Go. Each word is built first, by dragging sound tiles into the boxes, then traced.
  • Words are split into sounds automatically, so "night" becomes n · igh · t. To split a word your own way, put dots between the sounds, like h.o.t.h.ou.se. Learners don't see the dots.
  • The ☰ menu sets how much help each word gets when tracing, whether the word is shown while building, whether extra look-alike tiles appear, and turns the voice on or off.
  • There's no failing and no timer. Wrong tiles just wiggle and stay where they were dropped.
  • Works best on an iPad, with an Apple Pencil if you have one. Use Open full screen for the best fit.

Privacy

No student data leaves the device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Tick Tock Matching Game

Twelve clocks, twelve digital times, one line each. A worksheet plus two extension sheets for fast finishers.

Objective: Reading o'clock, quarter past, half past and quarter to on an analog clock and matching each to its digital time

  1. Worksheet

    Six clocks down each side and twelve digital times in the middle. Students draw a line from each time to the clock that shows it.

    Download worksheet

  2. How to use it

    • Do the first time together. Ask which clocks it could be, then which one it is and why.
    • Students work through the rest, crossing off each clock as they use it. Every clock is used exactly once, so the last few can be found by elimination.
    • Check by reading each clock aloud as a class.

  3. Extension 1

    A fresh set of twelve times for fast finishers. Same four types: o'clock, quarter past, half past and quarter to.

    Download extension 1

  4. Extension 2

    A third set. Includes 12:45 and 1:45, where the hour hand sits just before the 1 or the 2.

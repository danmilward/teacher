Sky Clock

Drag the hands and watch the sky change. The clock reads the time back in numbers and in words, and sets you a time to make.

Objective: Reading and setting o'clock, half past, quarter past and quarter to on an analog clock, and connecting the time to the part of the day

Open full screen

How to use it

  • Drag the red hour hand or the blue minute hand. Each hand can also be focused with the keyboard and moved with the arrow keys.
  • Press Spin the hands to whirl both hands like a prize wheel. Where they land is the time to read. The challenge level sets what it can land on.
  • Press Run clockwise and slide the speed to watch a whole day go past, sun and moon included.
  • Turn on Minute numbers to show 5, 10, 15 around the outside while students are learning to count the minutes.
  • The challenge at the bottom asks for a time. Start on the hour, then move up through half hours, five minutes and any minute.

Going from Analog to Digital

A printable worksheet for Year 2 students to practise reading analog clocks and writing the time in digital format and in words.

Objective: Reading quarter past, half past and quarter to on an analog clock and writing the time in digital format and in words

  1. Worksheet

    Five analog clocks showing quarter past, half past and quarter to. For each one, students write the time on the digital clock and again in words in the box.

    Download worksheet

  2. How to use it

    • Do the first row together. Say where the long hand is, say the time in words, then write it as digits.
    • Students complete the remaining four rows on their own or in pairs.
    • Check as a class. Ask students to explain how they knew, especially for quarter to.

    Pairs well with the Quarter Past and Half Past template for modelling first.

  3. Extension

    A second sheet with five new times for students who finish early. It includes quarter to 12 and quarter to 1, where the hour hand sits just before the 12 or the 1, which catches out students who read the hour hand too quickly.

Letter Wand

A phonics app I first made to help my son learn his letters and spelling. Trace letters with a magic wand, drag letters to spell picture words, then trace whole words and sentences. The help fades as children get more confident: follow the wand, then the dots, then on their own.

Objective: Forming letters correctly, recognising the letters in a word and putting them in order, and writing whole words and sentences

Open full screen

How to use it

  • Practise the alphabet: pick a letter, follow the wand to trace it, then trace it again with no help.
  • Spell words: choose a category (animals, food, emotions, sport and more), then drag the letters into the word in order.
  • Trace words: type a list of words separated by commas and press Go. Each word is traced in up to three rounds: with the wand, with just the starting dots, then on your own.
  • Trace sentences: type one sentence per line. Each word is traced on its own, with the whole sentence shown above. Capital letters and punctuation are traced too.
  • For grown-ups: make your own word lists for this week's words or a topic. They're saved on that device only.
  • Works best on an iPad, with an Apple Pencil if you have one. Use Open full screen for the best fit.

Privacy

No student data leaves the device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
